About the display: The SSD1306 128x64 blue/yellow version has the top 16 pixel rows in yellow and the remaining 48 rows in blue. This is physical—you can't change it in software. The ESPHome config takes advantage of this by putting the header/status in the yellow zone and the main drag info in the blue zone.

📍 OLED + Pot Wiring

GPIO21 (SDA) → OLED SDA pin
GPIO22 (SCL) → OLED SCL pin
3.3V → OLED VCC pin
GND → OLED GND pin
GPIO34 → Potentiometer wiper (middle pin)
3.3V → Potentiometer high side
GND → Potentiometer low side
