Wiring Summary

✓ Simplified Wiring

The MBR30100CT makes this easier—one part instead of two separate diodes, and the common cathode naturally connects to your +48V rail. Just three connections:

  • Left pin (1) → Q1 Drain + Coil 1 negative
  • Center pin (2) → +48V rail
  • Right pin (3) → Q2 Drain + Coil 2 negative

⚠️ Double-Check Before Powering On

  • Center pin MUST go to +48V (not ground!)
  • If wired backwards, the diodes will conduct constantly and short out
  • The tab/heatsink on the MBR30100CT is connected to the center pin (cathode) internally
