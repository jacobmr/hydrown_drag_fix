RESISTOR PLACEMENT DETAIL

330Ω Gate Resistor + 10K Pull-Down

Single MOSFET Circuit (×2 for both coils)

Complete Dual-Channel Circuit

💡 Summary

For each MOSFET channel:

  • 330Ω goes IN SERIES between ESP32 GPIO and the gate
  • 10K goes from the gate to ground (at the junction after the 330Ω)
  • Both resistors share a common connection point at the MOSFET gate

⚠️ Why Both Resistors Matter

  • 330Ω: Limits the current spike when charging/discharging the gate capacitance. Protects the ESP32 GPIO.
  • 10K: Keeps the gate at 0V when the ESP32 isn't actively driving it (during boot, reset, or if the ESP32 crashes). Without this, the coils could turn on unexpectedly.
